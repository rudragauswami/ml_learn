COMPANY POLICY AND KNOWLEDGE BASE

---
Section 1: Returns and Exchanges 
---
Return Timeline:
Customers may return most new, unopened items sold by our company within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The refund process generally takes 3-5 business days from the time the returned item is received at our fulfillment center.

Exceptions to the 30-day Return Policy:
- Electronics, such as televisions and computers, must be returned within 14 days of delivery.
- Software purchases, download codes, and gift cards are strictly non-refundable and cannot be exchanged. 
- Custom-built or personalized products are final sale unless they arrive defective or damaged.

Exchange Process:
To exchange an item, customers must initiate an RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) via the customer portal. The new item will not ship until the old item is scanned by the shipping carrier.

---
Section 2: Warranty Information
---
Standard Limited Warranty:
All hardware products come with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. This warranty does not cover accidental damage, improper use, or normal wear and tear.

Extended Protection Plan:
Customers who purchase the "Shield Plus" extended protection plan receive 3 years of total coverage, which includes accidental damage such as liquid spills or drops. Shield Plus claims have a flat $49 deductible per incident.

---
Section 3: Shipping
---
Shipping Costs:
Orders over $50 qualify for free standard ground shipping within the contiguous United States. Orders under $50 are subject to a flat $5.99 shipping fee.

Processing Time:
In-stock items typically ship within 1-2 business days. Expedited shipping options (Overnight, 2-Day) are available at checkout and guarantee priority fulfillment.
